# Code of Conduct

Quorum is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests or discussions) is expected to:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience level.
- Assume good intent, and give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and personal attacks are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove comments,
close threads or block participants who don't follow these guidelines.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub: [@dv333](https://github.com/dv333).

These guidelines are adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
